Press <kbd>⌘2</kbd>.

The Ascent is your journal seen from four heights:

- **Valley** — a week. Close enough to remember the days.
- **Hillside** — a month.
- **Ridge** — a quarter. Far enough that a season starts to have edges.
- **Summit** — a year.

Move between them and the same life resolves differently, the way a landscape
does as you climb.

## What you'll find at each height

**Arcs** — the things that ran through the period rather than happening once.

**Tensions** — the places where two things you wrote sat against each other.
Not resolved for you, just noticed.

**A refrain** — a line you wrote, quoted exactly, that carried the period.

## Where it comes from

Everything here is built from your own entries. The counting, the dating, the
grouping — all done in code, from facts. The only thing the model does is
**choose which of your sentences to show you**, and it quotes them verbatim.

It won't tell you what your year meant. It shows you your year and lets you be
the one who understands it.

## It needs a little time

A week needs a few entries to be worth looking at; a year needs a year. If a
height looks thin, it's usually because there isn't much there yet rather than
because something is broken.

If you've [imported an old journal](/help/bring-your-journal), your history gets
processed in the background after the import — so the higher altitudes may take
a little while to fill in the first time. It carries on without you.
