**Settings → Appearance.**

## Light, dark, or auto

Auto follows your device — light in the day, dark at night, if that's how you
have things set. It's the default.

## A theme is more than a colour

There are {{counts.voices}} themes, and each one is a whole look: the colours,
the typeface you write in, the way headings are set, and a small piece of
decoration that belongs to that theme alone.

Dawn is the default — sunrise on paper. Vellum is aged paper set in one
Garamond throughout, with the scripture reference picked out in red the way a
scribe would have done it. Cloister is cool stone and north light. Sabbath is
the quiet one, with the widest line spacing and no decoration at all. Plainsong
is plain text, in a monospace, for people who like it that way. Vigil is dimmed
right down for reading in a dark room.

Each theme comes with **both** a light and a dark version, so nothing jumps when
the sun goes down — pick Vellum once and you get Vellum by day and Vellum by
night. Two of the night versions are near-black, which is worth trying if you
read in bed.

The full list is in the app, and each row is set in its own typeface so you can
see what you're choosing before you choose it.

## If you'd rather pick the font yourself

Your theme brings a typeface that was chosen alongside its colours, which is
why there's no separate font setting in front of you any more.

If you want a different one, open **Advanced typography** underneath the theme
list. Six faces are there — a serif, a literary face, a typewriter face, a
monospace, a plain sans, and one designed for maximum legibility if small type
is a struggle — plus **Follow the theme**, which is where it starts.

Choosing one overrides the face only. Everything else about your theme stays.

This changes your writing, not the interface.

## Size and spacing

**Settings → Writing** has the text size, line spacing, and how wide the
writing column runs. Your size carries across themes — a theme adjusts it
rather than replacing it, so Plainsong's monospace comes out smaller than
Dawn's serif at the same setting, because monospace runs wider and needs it.

## Settings follow you

Everything here is stored with your account, so setting it up on your Mac sets
it up on your phone. There's no per-device configuration to keep in step.

If you ever want to start over, **Settings → About** has a reset that puts every
setting back to its default. It doesn't touch your entries.
