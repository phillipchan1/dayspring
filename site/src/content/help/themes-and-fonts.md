**Settings → Appearance.**

## Light, dark, or auto

Auto follows your device — light in the day, dark at night, if that's how you
have things set. It's the default.

## Two palettes, not one

You pick a **light** palette and a **dark** palette separately, and Dayspring
uses whichever matches. So you can have warm parchment by day and something
quiet by night without changing anything.

There are {{counts.themes}} in total. The light ones run from a sunrise
parchment to a cool north-light grey; the dark ones from moonlit to hearth-warm
to indigo. One of them is true black, for OLED screens — worth trying if you
read in bed.

The full list with descriptions is in the app; each has a one-line blurb and a
colour chip.

## The writing face

Six fonts for your entries — a serif that's the default, a literary face, a
typewriter face, a monospace, a plain sans, and one designed for maximum
legibility if small type is a struggle.

This changes your writing, not the interface.

## Settings follow you

Everything here is stored with your account, so setting it up on your Mac sets
it up on your phone. There's no per-device configuration to keep in step.

If you ever want to start over, **Settings → About** has a reset that puts every
setting back to its default. It doesn't touch your entries.
