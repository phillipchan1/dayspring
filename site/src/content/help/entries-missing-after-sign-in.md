Take a breath. This is the most common thing people write to us about, and in
almost every case nothing has been lost.

## What's happened

Dayspring signs you in with Apple or with Google. Those are two different
identities. If you used one the first time and the other the second time, you're
now looking at a **second, brand-new account** — which is, correctly, empty.

Apple's **Hide My Email** makes this easier to hit. It gives us a relay address
like `x7k2p9@privaterelay.appleid.com` instead of your real one, so there's no
way for us to see that it's the same person as your Google sign-in.

## The fix

1. **Sign out.**
2. **Sign back in the way you did the first time** — the other button.
3. Your journal is there.

If you can't remember which you used first, try the other one. There are only
two.

## Then stop it happening again

Once you're in the right account: **Settings → About**, find your sign-in
methods, and **connect the other provider**.

After that either button gets you to the same journal, and this can't recur.

## If both accounts have writing in them

That happens when someone writes for a while before noticing. Don't delete
either one.

[Write to us](/help/contact) and say so. Tell us both email addresses if you
know them, or roughly when you started each. We'll merge them into one account
with everything intact.

## If your subscription is on the wrong one

Also fixable, and also something to tell us about. An Apple subscription can
only entitle one account, so it needs moving rather than re-buying — **don't
subscribe a second time.**
