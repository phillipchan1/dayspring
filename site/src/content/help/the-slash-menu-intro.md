Type `/` at the start of a line, or after a space, and a menu opens.

It won't fire in the middle of a word, or inside a URL, or in "and/or" — so you
can write about `https://example.com/page` without a menu jumping out at you.

## Two columns

**Format** does something to your text straight away: a heading, a list, a
quote, bold, a divider.

**Capture** opens a small window to fill in: a passage of scripture, a prayer, a
photo, one of the contemplative practices.

## Filtering

Keep typing after the `/` and the list narrows. You don't have to know the exact
name — `/verse` finds Scripture, `/practice` finds Ritual, `/check` finds
To-do.

Arrow keys to move, <kbd>Return</kbd> to choose, <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close.

## On a phone or tablet

There's no slash key convenient on a touch keyboard, so the mid-sentence
commands sit in a row of buttons directly above it — scripture, prayer, sense,
image, emoji, and an **Aa** button that swaps the row for formatting.

**Ritual** is different: it is a shape for the whole page, so on a blank page it
sits up top next to the title (same as on a computer). Once you have written
something, reach it from the `+` beside a line, or type `/ritual`.

That keyboard bar is also where [voice dictation](/help/dictating-an-entry) and
[page scanning](/help/scanning-handwriting) live.
