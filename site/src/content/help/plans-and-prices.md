There's one plan. Everything is in it. You choose how often you pay.

## On the web and on your Mac

- **{{billing.web.annual}} a year** — works out around $5.33 a month.
- **{{billing.web.monthly}} a month.**

Both start with a [{{billing.trial-days}}-day trial](/help/your-trial), and no
card is needed to begin.

Payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.

## On iPhone and iPad

Prices there are set through the App Store and are slightly different — Apple's
price tiers end in .99, so the figures don't match the web exactly. The app
always shows you the real App Store price for your country and currency, in your
own money.

Purchases on iPhone go through Apple, as Apple requires.

## Which one you're on

**Settings → Billing** tells you plainly: the plan, whether it's billed through
the App Store or on the web, and the date it renews.

## A note on paying twice

One subscription covers your account everywhere — write on your Mac, your
phone, and the web on the same one.

If you already subscribe through Apple, Dayspring won't offer to sell you
another subscription on the web. It'll offer to *manage* the one you have
instead. See [managing your
subscription](/help/managing-your-subscription).

## Why it costs anything

Dayspring is a subscription because it does work for you every month — reading
your journal back, building the Ascent, keeping the Lamp lit, and storing your
writing and your photos safely.

There's no free tier with your history locked behind it, and there's no version
that sells your writing to anyone. See [what the AI does and
doesn't do](/help/what-the-ai-does).
