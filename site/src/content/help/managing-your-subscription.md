**Settings → Billing → Manage billing.** That button knows where to send you.

## Which one applies to you

It depends on **who took the money**, not which device you're holding.

- Subscribed on **iPhone or iPad**? Apple bills you, and managing it means
  going to your Apple subscriptions — the button opens them for you.
- Subscribed on the **web or your Mac**? Stripe bills you, and the button opens
  the Stripe billing portal.

If you subscribed on the web and you're holding your iPhone, you'll still be
sent to Stripe. That's correct, even though it feels odd — Apple has no record
of a subscription it never sold.

## Cancelling

Cancel in whichever of those two places applies. You keep access until the end
of the period you've already paid for; nothing is cut off mid-month.

Your entries aren't deleted when a subscription ends. And you can
[download a complete backup](/help/download-a-backup) at any point, including
after it lapses.

## Restore purchases

On iPhone and iPad there's a **Restore purchases** button. Use it after
reinstalling, or on a new device, if Dayspring doesn't recognise that you've
already paid.

It tells you in plain words what it found, rather than silently doing nothing.

## Switching monthly to yearly

Change the plan in the same place you manage billing — Apple's subscription
settings, or the Stripe portal.

## If you've just paid and nothing happened

Give it a few seconds. If it takes longer, Dayspring shows you a screen saying
your payment went through and it's still waiting to hear back — with a **Check
again** button. It won't leave you looking at a spinner wondering whether you've
been charged.

If it's still stuck after that, [tell us](/help/contact) and include the email
address you paid with.

## One subscription, one account

An Apple subscription can only entitle one Dayspring account. If you've ended up
with [two accounts](/help/entries-missing-after-sign-in), the subscription stays
with the one that bought it — get in touch and we'll sort it out.
