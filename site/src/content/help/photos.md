Three ways to get a photo onto a page:

- **Drag** it from Finder or your desktop straight into the entry.
- **Paste** it — copy an image anywhere and press <kbd>⌘V</kbd>.
- Type **`/image`** and pick a file.

Each image can be up to **{{limits.image-max-mb}} MB**. Larger ones are
compressed on your device before they're uploaded, so a photo straight off a
modern phone is fine.

## Once it's on the page

Click a photo and a menu opens: change its size, add a caption or alt text, or
remove it.

Photos are objects rather than text, so a click opens that menu rather than
putting a cursor into it. Drag one to move it somewhere else in the entry — a
line shows where it'll land.

## Offline

Add photos on a plane if you like. They're held on your device and uploaded when
you're back online, retrying by themselves. The entry is readable the whole
time.

If a photo seems stuck, see [photos not
uploading](/help/photos-not-uploading).

## Photos are in your backup

When you [download a backup](/help/download-a-backup), the image files come with
it — not links to images, the actual files. Your journal doesn't become a set of
broken picture icons if you ever stop using Dayspring.
