Press <kbd>⌘4</kbd>.

Every prayer you log with `/pray` comes here. The Altar gathers them by subject
— a person, a situation, a fear you keep bringing back — so you can see a thing
you've been praying about across months rather than one entry at a time.

## Two views

**Subjects** shows the field of what you've been praying about, and how often
each has come up.

**Over time** shows the same thing chronologically — when something first
appeared, when you returned to it, when it stopped.

## What came of it

This is the part people don't expect. Alongside a prayer, the Altar shows what
you wrote about that same subject afterwards — in ordinary entries, not marked
as anything in particular.

You prayed about a conversation you were dreading. Six weeks later you wrote a
sentence about how it went, and thought nothing of it. The Altar puts those two
next to each other.

## No ledger

There's no answered/unanswered column, no counter, no percentage.

That's a deliberate refusal. Some of what you'll see here is a prayer and its
answer sitting side by side. Some is a prayer you prayed for two years with
nothing after it. Both are true things about your life, and neither is a score.

Light, not verdict — that's the rule the whole app is built on.
