Type `/ritual` and the practice library opens.

These are old forms of prayer, laid out as prompts you write into. The Daily
Examen. Lectio Divina. Wesley's Questions. SOAP. A psalmic lament for when you
need one. The Round, once a week, walks the domains of your own life one at a
time — what's true here, this week.

## Choosing one

The library opens on what the hour already suggests — mornings lead with the
rituals that begin a day, evenings with the ones that close it — and says why
on screen. Search by name, tradition, or a line that's stuck with you, or just
browse.

If you're new to this, the **Daily Examen** in the evening is the usual door in.

## What happens on the page

Pick one and its prompts appear as guiding questions, each with a line to write
on.

**Only what you type is saved.** The prompts are scaffolding — they're not
stored in your entry, and they don't appear in your exports. If you answer two
of four questions and stop, your entry is two paragraphs, not an abandoned form.

You can also ignore the prompts entirely and just write. Nothing enforces the
shape.

## The introduction screen

Before the prompts, you get a short page on where the practice comes from and
how it moves — worth reading the first few times.

Once you know them, turn it off in Settings → Writing and go straight to the
page.

## It won't grade you

No completion tracking, no streaks, no gentle reminder that it's been a while
since your last Examen. Use one every day or twice a year.
