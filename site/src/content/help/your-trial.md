When you sign in for the first time you get **{{billing.trial-days}} days**,
with everything switched on.

No card. No "start trial" button to hunt for. No billing details taken up front
and quietly charged on day fifteen. You sign in and you're writing.

## What's included

All of it. The editor and every slash command, the Lamp, the Altar, the Ascent
across every horizon, photos, importing your old journal — there is no reduced
trial version of Dayspring.

That's deliberate. A journal that shows you your own life over time can't
demonstrate what it's for in a crippled version.

## Where to see how long is left

**Settings → Billing** shows the days remaining and the exact date it ends,
along with a plain line confirming there's no charge until then.

## When it ends

You'll be asked to subscribe. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is held hostage —
you can still **download a complete backup** of everything you wrote, right from
the screen that asks you to subscribe. Your writing is yours whether or not you
pay us.

## If {{billing.trial-days}} days wasn't enough

There's a **Take another week** option on that same screen, worth
{{billing.extension-days}} more days. Press it and keep going.

It's once per account, and it's self-serve — you don't have to email anyone or
explain yourself. Some months are just busy.

If you need longer than that, [ask us](/help/contact). We'd rather you decided
properly than decided in a hurry.
