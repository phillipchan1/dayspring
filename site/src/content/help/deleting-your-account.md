**Settings → About → Delete account.** It's at the bottom, under Account
Actions. If your subscription has lapsed and you're looking at the locked
screen instead, the same link is there too.

## Before anything else, take your writing with you

The delete panel has a **Take a backup first** button, and it's there because
you only get one chance to think of it. You get a `.zip` containing every entry
as plain text and every photo as a real image file — the same backup as
[Download a backup](/help/download-a-backup).

Deletion is permanent. We can't get it back for you afterwards, and neither can
anyone else.

## Cancel an App Store subscription first

If Apple bills you, **deleting your account won't stop that** — only you can,
in the App Store. Apple gives us no way to cancel on your behalf.

So Dayspring won't delete your account while your App Store subscription is
still set to renew. It says so, and gives you a button to
[manage it at Apple](/help/managing-your-subscription). Turn off renewal, then
come back and delete. You keep the time you've already paid for; Apple simply
won't charge you again.

If **Stripe** bills you — you subscribed on the web or your Mac — you don't
need to do anything. Dayspring cancels it for you as part of the deletion.

## Deleting

Type `DELETE` in the box and press **Delete my account**. Nothing happens until
you do; the button stays greyed out.

Then it's done — immediately, not in seven days. You're signed out, and the copy
held on that device is scrubbed too.

## What gets deleted

Your entries and your photos, and everything built from them — the Lamp, the
Altar, the Ascent, your Concordance, your marks — and the account itself.

## What we can't delete

Apple and Stripe keep a record of payments you made. They're required to, for
tax and legal reasons, and they don't give us a way to erase it. Those are
billing records held by them, not your writing, and nothing in them is anything
you wrote.

Dayspring itself keeps nothing after deletion — not your email, not a note that
the account ever existed.

## If something goes wrong

If Dayspring can't cancel your Stripe subscription, or can't reach Apple to
check whether yours is still renewing, it stops and deletes nothing rather than
leaving you paying for a journal that no longer exists. Try again in a few
minutes; if it keeps failing,
[get in touch](mailto:hello@usedayspring.app) and we'll sort it out.
