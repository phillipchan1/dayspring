## Check the size

The limit is **{{limits.image-max-mb}} MB** per image. Dayspring compresses
large photos on your device first, so this is rarely the problem — but a raw
file or a long screenshot of a whole web page can exceed it.

## Check the connection

Photos upload in the background and retry by themselves. If you added one
offline, it goes when you're next connected with the app open.

The entry is readable the whole time. A photo that hasn't finished uploading
never blocks your writing.

## Still stuck

1. Give it a minute with the app open and connected — retries are automatic.
2. Delete the photo from the entry and add it again.
3. If it's an unusual format, open it in Preview or Photos and export it as JPEG
   or PNG first.

## What isn't supported

Images only. Audio files, video and PDFs can't be attached to an entry.

If you [imported from Day One](/help/bring-your-journal), that's worth knowing:
your photos came across, but audio recordings and PDFs didn't.

## Photos are in your backup

When you [download a backup](/help/download-a-backup), the actual image files
are in the zip. If a photo is showing in your entries, it's safe.

Still not right? [Send us the details](/help/contact) — the file size and format
help.
