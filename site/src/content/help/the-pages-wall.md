Press <kbd>⌘1</kbd>.

This is every entry you've written, laid out as pages rather than as a list of
dates. Stand back and you see years at once; come close and you're reading.

## Zooming

Pinch, or hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> and scroll, or use <kbd>⌘=</kbd> and <kbd>⌘−</kbd>.
There's a slider too.

It's continuous rather than three fixed sizes, so you can sit wherever the view
becomes useful to you. Far out, the shape of your writing life is visible —
the dense months, the silences. Close in, the near end reads like an open book.

## The Spread

Press <kbd>Return</kbd> on a page to open it two-up, like a book, with room in
the margin.

## Filtering

Chips along the top narrow the wall, and they combine. Show only entries you've
marked. Only ones with scripture in them. Only prayers. Only pages you
highlighted.

Chips you have nothing for don't appear, so you're never offered an empty
filter.

There's also a filter box that takes plain English — "entries about my mother
from last spring". It sets the filters for you; it does not decide what matches.
The distinction matters: the model can configure the search, but the results are
always literally your entries meeting literal criteria.

## Weather

A panel of the plain facts: how many pages, the first, the most recent, your
longest silence, and a grid of the months.

The longest silence is often the interesting one.

## Anniversaries

As you scroll, pages from this date in earlier years are folded in — not as a
notification, just there in the flow, where you'd come across them the way you'd
come across an old letter in a drawer.

## Keyboard

Arrow keys move between pages. <kbd>Shift</kbd> with arrows extends a selection,
<kbd>⌘A</kbd> selects everything, <kbd>Return</kbd> reads, <kbd>Esc</kbd>
clears.

With pages selected you can copy them, export them as Markdown, or delete them
in one go.
