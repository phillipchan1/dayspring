Cards expire. Banks decline things for no reason anyone can explain. This should
not be the moment you lose access to your journal.

## What happens

If a renewal fails, **Settings → Billing** shows a payment issue, and you keep
your access for a few days' grace while it sorts itself out.

That window exists for a real reason: sometimes the payment is fine and it's our
notification from Stripe or Apple that got lost. Cutting someone off from their
own journal because of a dropped webhook would be indefensible.

## Fixing it

**Settings → Billing → Manage billing** takes you to the right place to update
your card — Apple's subscription settings, or the Stripe portal. See
[managing your subscription](/help/managing-your-subscription).

Once the payment clears, access continues without a gap. Press the refresh
arrow beside your plan if you want it to check straight away rather than waiting.

## If access does stop

You'll see the subscribe screen. Two things are still true:

1. **Nothing has been deleted.** Your entries are exactly where you left them,
   and they come back the moment a payment clears.
2. **You can still download a complete backup**, right from that screen. Your
   writing does not become hostage to a billing problem.

## Still stuck

[Write to us](/help/contact) with the email address on the account. If this is a
mess of our making, we'll fix it and extend you the time you lost.
