Press <kbd>⌘3</kbd>.

The Lamp is the whole Bible laid out as a map, with each book lit according to
how often your writing has touched it. Books you've lived in glow. Books you
haven't stay dark.

## How it fills in

Every time you save an entry, Dayspring notices Bible references in it — whether
you used `/scripture` or just typed "Romans 8:28" in a sentence. Those references
light the map.

Nothing to tag, nothing to file.

## What it's for

After a year, the Lamp is a fairly honest picture of where you've actually been.
Most people find it shows something they half-knew and had never seen laid out:
a season spent almost entirely in the Psalms, or a whole Testament barely
touched.

**It isn't a scorecard.** A dark book is not a failing, and there's no coverage
percentage anywhere. It's a map of where you've been, and a map is only useful
because it shows you where you haven't gone yet.

## Going deeper

Click a book to open it. You'll see a strip of its chapters, a timeline of when
you wrote near it, and the threads of your own writing around particular verses.

Passages you keep returning to show up in a returning strip — the verses that
have followed you.

## If it looks emptier than your life

You've probably just imported an old journal. Imported entries bypass the
editor, so their references haven't been noticed yet.

Run **Scan journal for references** in the scripture settings once, and the map
fills in. See [importing](/help/bring-your-journal).
