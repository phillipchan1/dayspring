Press <kbd>⌘⏎</kbd>. The navigation, the sidebar, and everything else that isn't
your writing fades out.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to come back.

## Two settings inside it

While you're in focus mode, two small controls float nearby.

**Typewriter scrolling** keeps the line you're writing vertically centred,
instead of letting it sink to the bottom of the screen. Your eyes stay in one
place. On by default.

**Dimming** fades the paragraphs you aren't currently in, so the one you're
writing is the one you can read clearly. Also on by default.

Both are toggles — try them off if they're not for you. They're also in
Settings → Writing if you'd rather set them once.

## The rest of the reading experience

Font, size, line height and column width all live in Settings → Writing. The
column width one is worth a look: narrowing it makes a page read much more like
a book and much less like a document.

See [writing settings](/help/writing-settings).
