**Settings → Writing.**

## The three sliders

**Font size** runs from {{editor.font-size-min}} to {{editor.font-size-max}}
pixels, and starts at {{editor.font-size-default}}. That default is larger than
most apps on purpose — you're writing, not filling in a form.

**Line height** controls the air between lines.

**Column width** is the one people miss. It sets how wide the writing column
runs. Narrow it and a page starts reading like a book; leave it wide and it
reads like a document. If Dayspring doesn't quite feel right and you can't say
why, try this first.

## First line as title

On by default. The first line of an entry becomes its title — in the app, and in
anything you export.

Turn it off if you'd rather your entries just start.

## Show markdown syntax

Off by default, which is why you see **bold text** rather than `**bold text**`.
Turn it on if you'd rather see the raw characters. See
[formatting](/help/formatting-and-markdown).

## Practice previews

On by default. When you choose a contemplative practice, you get a short
introduction before the prompts appear. Turn it off to go straight to writing
once you know them.

## Typewriter and dimming

**Typewriter scrolling** keeps the line you're writing in the middle of the page.
Choose **Off**, **In focus** (the default), or **Always**. It's there on a Mac,
in a browser, and on an iPad with a keyboard; on iPhone it isn't offered.

**Paragraph dimming** fades the paragraphs you aren't in, in focus mode.

Both are explained in [focus mode](/help/focus-mode).
