# Dayspring — Messaging & One-Pager Copy

## The strategic spine (read this first)

**Refuse two categories.** Don't compete in "AI journaling" (a therapy-chatbot
arms race — mood trackers with a chat skin) or "Christian journaling" (a
commodity pile of verse-of-the-day, prayer logs, and cartoon-mascot habit
streaks). Both are races to the bottom.

**Own a third thing: time.** Dayspring is the only journal built around the
long view — *who you're becoming* over months and years, not how you feel today.

**The one idea everything ladders up to:**
> A day is too small to hold a pattern. A year is a mirror.

**How a believer knows it's for them — without a single cringe.** Three quiet
signals, never a banner:
1. **The name.** Dayspring (Luke 1:78). A believer hears it; everyone else just
   hears "dawn."
2. **Precise language, used naturally** — *becoming, formation, seasons, head to
   heart, held in confidence, before it's ever spoken aloud.* Never religious
   filler.
3. **Exactly one unmistakable line,** placed with confidence: *"It's just
   between you and God."* Restraint is the whole game. The lame apps shout;
   Dayspring lets a believer recognize their own.

## THE ONE-PAGER (top to bottom, paste-ready)

### Hero
**See who you're becoming.**

A private journal that turns what you write each day into a year-long mirror —
so you can finally see how far you've come, not just how you feel today.

### The problem (one line — sets up the wedge)
Every other journal keeps you in today: today's mood, today's prompt, today's
verse. None of them show you the arc.

### Section 1 — The editor (proof it isn't lame)
**A page worth returning to.**

No clutter, no nagging, no cartoon streaks. A beautiful full-screen page that
gets out of your way — typewriter scrolling, the themes and fonts you'd expect
in a code editor, and zero lag between thought and word. The kind of page you
actually want to open.

### Section 2 — The hero: temporal synthesis
**Letters to yourself, not reports.**

Each week, month, and year, Dayspring reads back what you wrote and tells you
what changed — in your own story, not a chart. *"Here's what shifted in you this
month."* By year's end you'll have something almost no one keeps: an honest
mirror of who you were twelve months ago, and who you've become.

### Section 3 — Formation (the unmistakable, tasteful tell)
**Track the things that never make it onto a to-do list.**

Are you growing in patience? Is your prayer life deepening? Is what you believe
finally moving from your head to your heart? These are the changes that matter
most and are hardest to see day to day. Dayspring watches for them over time,
gently — and shows you the movement.

### Section 4 — The Gain (the emotional reframe)
**Measure backward, not forward.**

Most of us measure against the person we think we should be — and always come up
short. Dayspring measures against who you actually were. You'll be surprised how
far grace has carried you.

### Section 5 — Bring your history (cold-start killer)
**Years of journaling, brought with you.**

Already keep a journal in Day One or Diarly? Import it, and Dayspring hands you a
look back at last month — and last year — on your very first day. Nothing starts
from scratch.

### Section 6 — Privacy as stewardship
**Some things are meant to be written before they're ever spoken aloud.**

Your entries are encrypted, never sold, and never used to train AI. No one else
can reach your journal. And when something is too tender even for that, turn on
the Private Vault — locked with a key only you hold. We can't read it. Neither
can the AI. It's just between you and God.

The page is free. The mirror comes with time.

## Language guardrails

**Use:** becoming · formation · seasons · the arc · a year is a mirror · held in
confidence · stewardship · tender · who you were vs. who you are · grace ·
carried · the long view.

**Ban (instant lame):** "journaling app," "devotional," "verse of the day,"
"faith journey," "spiritual wellness," streaks/badges/mascots, exclamation-heavy
hype, "unlock your best self," "level up your walk," emoji clutter.

**Also avoid the secular tell:** "emotional patterns," "mental fitness,"
"cognitive biases," "your AI therapist." That's the lane you're *beating*, not
joining.
