Write for a while and patterns start to show that you’d never notice one page at a time. Dayspring gathers them in three places, and all of it is in your own words.

The Ascent. Your week, month, season and year, told back from what you actually wrote: what kept coming back and what went quiet. Every line is quoted exactly. If you didn’t write it, it isn’t there.

The Lamp. Every passage of Scripture you’ve written about, laid across the whole Bible, so you can see where your heart has been leaning.

The Altar. Your prayers, gathered by the people and things you keep bringing to God.

All three fill in as you write, so if they look quiet right now, that’s expected. They’re made for the long view.
