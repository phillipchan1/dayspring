Most of what we write, we never read again. That isn’t because it didn’t matter. There was just no way back in.

In Dayspring, Journal is that way back in. Every entry is laid out as a page, newest first. Step back to see months at a glance, or open any page to read it.

The part worth knowing is Look for. Type a name or a word you carry, like Dad, work or Psalm 23, and only the pages that mention it stay lit. Choose prayer and you’ll see every page where you prayed.

On the Mac, ⌘K finds any word from anywhere in the app.
