Marking a phrase inside a verse

You can mark a sentence you wrote. You cannot mark a line of scripture — the block is one button, and its words refuse to be selected at all.

This walks through a fix. Four screens, a few minutes. Use it on your phone as well as your laptop — the two gestures are different and both have to feel obvious.

What I'm asking

  • Does it stay clear which part opens the chapter and which part takes a mark?
  • Does one button feel right, or do you reach for bold?
  • On your phone: does press-and-hold arrive where you expect it?

Not the live app. Chapter text here is public-domain (WEB) standing in for ESV, same as the scripture prototype.

Screen 3 of 3 · coming back

What you marked, months later

Marks already have a home — Pages, filtered to Marked. A phrase from a verse arrives there like any other, with one difference: it carries its reference, so a line of scripture can never read as something you wrote.

What this does not do

No colours. No bold. Nothing counted, nothing ranked, and the Lamp is unchanged — it still says which verses kept finding you, not which words did. That's a later question, and a bigger one.

Last screen

Your read

Three things worth saying out loud before any of this gets built:

  • Did you ever click the words expecting the chapter, or the citation expecting a selection? Which way did you guess wrong?
  • The hairline and the arrow on the citation — enough, too much, or the wrong shape? They are the one thing here that adds ink to a pull-quote you see every day.
  • Did you want bold — really want it, not just notice it was missing?
  • On the phone: did press-and-hold occur to you, or did you have to be told?
If none of it was confusing and you never reached for bold, the rule holds and the next move is the probe: ship the counter from screen 1 into the real app for a week, and see whether anyone is reaching for this at all.
