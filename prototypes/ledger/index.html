The year's ledger · prototype

Each month closes a ledger. The year is their sum.

No model decides what mattered. When a month ends, code counts what you wrote about: which subjects (Altar threads, names, verses), how often, what moved (an answered prayer, a prayer that turned into something you learned), and what you marked yourself. The year adds up twelve ledgers without losing anything, so its shape changes as months arrive. Click a row to walk that thread through the year.
