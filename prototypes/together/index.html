How it fits together ▾

Pages: sections are volumes. Drag zoom all the way back and the pages fold into spines — the shelf. Look for a subject: up close it lights pages; on the shelf it puts a ribbon in every volume it runs through.

Read it back on a volume, or tap a thread, to see it told. Threads are chaptered by the volumes they ran through.

Ascent is the climb: This week (just pages) · September (what's alive) · Fall 2026 (what moved — real seasons) · 2026 (told back). Names are calendar-only unless you give one.

Journals look like (top bar) switches covers, flat notebooks, and the classic spines everywhere.
