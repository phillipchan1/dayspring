Privacy Policy

Dayspring · Last updated 11 August 2026

Dayspring is a private journal. What you write in it belongs to you. This policy describes exactly what we store, what leaves our systems, and what you can take back.

What we store

  • Account — your email address and the identity provider you signed in with (Google or Apple). We never receive or store your password for those providers.
  • Your journal — entries, photographs you attach, voice recordings you make, and the material Dayspring derives from them (reflections, prayer threads, scripture references).
  • Settings and usage state — theme, editor preferences, onboarding progress, and subscription status.

Journal data is stored in Postgres on Supabase infrastructure in the United States, and is protected by row-level security so that each account can read only its own rows.

Who else processes your data

Dayspring uses a small number of service providers. They act on our instructions and only receive what the feature requires:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel — application hosting and serverless functions.
  • OpenAI — when you dictate a voice note, photograph a handwritten page, or Dayspring builds a reflection, the relevant text, audio or image is sent to the OpenAI API for processing. OpenAI's API terms state that data submitted through the API is not used to train their models.
  • Crossway (ESV API) — scripture references you write are sent to resolve verse text. Only the reference is sent, never your entry.
  • Stripe — subscriptions purchased on the web. Stripe handles the card directly; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Apple — subscriptions purchased in the iOS app. Apple handles the payment; we receive only a transaction identifier and subscription status.
  • Resend — used to send reminder emails, if you enable them.
  • PostHog — product usage measurement, if you leave "Share usage data" switched on (Settings → About). Receives a fixed list of feature-usage events — which screens you open, which commands you use, whether an import or a purchase succeeded — identified by your account's internal ID. It never receives your entries, your prayers, anything you searched for, or any text you wrote. See below.

If you send feedback through the in-app feedback form, the message you type is recorded as an issue in our private development tracker so it can be acted on. Please do not paste journal content into that form.

Usage measurement

Dayspring records a small, fixed list of feature-usage events so we can tell which parts of the product are actually useful — for example that a Return surface was opened, that an import finished, or that a search was run. This is controlled by Settings → About → "Share usage data", which is on by default and which you can switch off at any time. Switching it off stops collection immediately and clears the identifiers stored on your device.

We want to be precise rather than reassuring here:

  • No text you wrote is ever included — not entries, not prayers, not scripture references you typed, not search or Ask questions, not file names. Every property of every event is a fixed value from a predefined list, a number, or a true/false flag. There is no free-text field in the system, so your words cannot travel through it even by mistake.
  • Counts are rounded into ranges. An import is recorded as "1,000–5,000 entries", never the exact number, because an exact number can identify a person.
  • This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Events are linked to your account's internal ID so that a returning reader isn't counted as a new one. They are never linked to anything you wrote.
  • We do not record your screen — no session replay, no heatmaps, no automatic capture of clicks or typing. Nothing observes the writing surface.
  • Web addresses are reduced to the page name before being sent, so nothing that may appear in a link — such as a sign-in token — is transmitted.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • We do not send your journal, or anything derived from its text, to any analytics or advertising service.
  • We do not use your journal to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not read your entries except where you explicitly ask for support and give permission, or where we are legally compelled to.

Your data is portable

You can export your entire journal as a ZIP archive at any time from within the app — including while your subscription is lapsed or cancelled. Your journal is always yours.

Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated journal data by contacting us at the address below. We will delete it from the live database promptly. Encrypted backups may retain copies for a short period before rotating out.

Retention

We keep your journal for as long as your account exists, because the value of the product is precisely that history. We do not delete entries when a subscription lapses.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Children

Dayspring is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your journal is handled, notify you in the app.

Contact
