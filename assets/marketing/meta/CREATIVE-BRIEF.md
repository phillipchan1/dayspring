# Dayspring Meta creative brief

## Objective

Use paid-social collateral as a positioning test, not as proof that acquisition is
ready to scale. The creative must make the category obvious in one glance and then
show why Dayspring is different.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Category:** Journaling for spiritual growth.
2. **Promise:** Write freely. Over time, see what God has been making of you.
3. **Proof:** Dayspring returns the writer's own prayers, passages, and seasons without
   inventing memories or scoring spiritual life.
4. **Action:** Begin your 14-day free trial.

The first frame always uses the exact category line. More poetic language such as
"the long view," "mirror," and "remembering" comes only after the offer is clear.

## Audience

Primary: practicing Christians who already journal and rarely reread what they wrote.
They may have years of paper, Day One, or Diarly history.

Secondary: reflective Christians who use journaling to notice where God met them but
find old entries too difficult or time-consuming to revisit.

## Evidence

- Judy described rereading as a way to see "where has God met me?" and remember
  breakthroughs, themes, and verses.
- Kristi described growth as slow and repetitive, and Dayspring as a new way to engage
  her heart with God.
- Both interviews favor reflection support over another habit-building system.

These are two strong-fit interviews, not broad market validation. No creative should
imply a user count, consensus, or testimonial endorsement.

## Four supporting directions

### 1. The Long View

- Hook: Spiritual growth is slow. See its shape over time.
- Proof: weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly reflection grounded in the writer's
  own entries.
- Visual: the Ascent and one verbatim line from the year.

### 2. The Journal That Remembers

- Hook: You wrote the prayer. Dayspring helps you remember what came after.
- Proof: prayers gather by the people and matters the writer carried.
- Visual: the Altar's warm strands.

### 3. Made for the Inner Life

- Hook: Beautiful to write in. Made for the inner life.
- Proof: Scripture, Prayer, Sense, and Ritual live on a quiet writing surface.
- Visual: the warm Dawn editor.

### 4. Your History, Read Back

- Hook: Ten years of journaling. One story you've never read.
- Proof: Day One and Diarly imports preserve original dates and make old entries
  available to the Return surfaces.
- Visual: archive history followed by the Ascent payoff.

## Production selection

Produce the first placement kit from:

1. **Journaling for spiritual growth / The Long View** — the clearest articulation of
   the user's desired category, paired with the strongest emotional payoff.
2. **Your History, Read Back** — the most concrete product-proof concept and the
   narrowest test of the Archivist acquisition hypothesis.

## Visual system

- Ink: `#0c0d11`
- Paper: `#ece7dd`
- Dawn amber: `#f3bd76`
- Dawn rose: `#e8917c`
- Deep rose: `#c56a6e`
- Gold: `#e4ba7c`
- Display: Fraunces
- Body: Newsreader
- Labels: JetBrains Mono

Use dawn light, quiet contrast, generous negative space, restrained grain, and real
product imagery. Avoid generic worship photography, crosses used as decoration,
stock prayer poses, badges, progress meters, and testimonial quotation marks.

## Copy guardrails

- Never use: AI-powered, insights, unlock, journey, optimize, streak, score,
  mindfulness, or spiritual wellness.
- Never promise a verdict on growth.
- Never imply end-to-end encryption.
- Never paraphrase fictional users as testimonials.
- Prefer: remember, faithfulness, season, carry, notice, your own words, and over time.

## Deliverables

- Direction board: mandatory opening plus four supporting 1080×1350 concepts.
- Placement kit: two selected concepts in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, and 1080×1920.
- Editable source: `template.html`, `styles.css`, and `copy.json`.
- Rebuild command: `npm run collateral:meta`.
